## Document Identified Issues

You should now have a list of at least 10 items you've flagged as potentially suspicious or problematic. In this exercise, you'll document these in more detail.
For each issue, create a record including:

- Relevant log snippets (IPs, ports, protocols, timestamps etc.)
- A name/title summarizing the issue
- An explanation of why it appears suspicious
- An analysis of the potential impact if it is indeed malicious
- Possible explanations or root causes (e.g. misconfiguration, malware, attack, etc.)

Organize these records in a document or spreadsheet. Aim to be thorough but concise. Prioritize the issues based on your judgement of severity and impact.

By the end of this exercise, you should have a well-structured document detailing at least 10 potential security issues you've uncovered through your analysis. This artifact demonstrates your ability to interpret the log data and connects it to real-world security concerns.
